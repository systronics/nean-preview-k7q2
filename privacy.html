Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Nean Healthcare (“Nean Healthcare”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we handle it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

1. Who we are

Nean Healthcare is a PCD franchise and third-party manufacturing company with its head office in Kala Amb, Himachal Pradesh and a corporate office in Mohali, Punjab. We decide why and how your personal data from this website is used.

2. What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, firm name, business type, city and state, phone number, email address, drug licence number, GST number, products or territory of interest, and any message you write.
  • Calls, WhatsApp and email: the details you share when you contact us directly.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited in its server logs.

We do not ask for patient or medical information. Please do not send it to us.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s session storage only to remember that you confirmed you are a healthcare professional or trade partner, for the length of your visit.

4. Why we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss PCD franchise or third-party manufacturing.
  • To verify that you are a licensed trade partner, which we may need to do before supplying prescription products.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory requirements.
  • To keep our records and improve our services.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the form, and for lawful purposes permitted by Indian law.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with service providers who help us run the website and handle enquiries (such as form, email, messaging and hosting services), with our manufacturing partners where needed to fulfil your request, and with government or regulatory authorities when the law requires. Some of these providers may store data outside India.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or longer where the law requires records to be kept (for example for tax and drug-licence purposes). After that we delete or anonymise it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including HTTPS on this website and limiting access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct or update inaccurate data;
  • ask us to erase your data, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, which will not affect earlier lawful use;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to do so;
  • complain to us, and if not resolved, to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

This website is meant for business and professional use and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Links to other sites

Our website may link to other sites, such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
