FAQ

Common questions

What is a PCD pharma franchise?

PCD (Propaganda cum Distribution) is a business model where a distributor or marketing firm promotes and sells a company's products in an allotted territory. Nean Healthcare supports PCD partners with marketing material and documentation.

Who can apply for a Nean Healthcare PCD franchise?

Distributors, wholesalers, chemists, pharma marketing firms and healthcare professionals who hold the licences needed to buy, stock and sell the products can send an enquiry.

Do you offer third-party manufacturing?

Yes. We offer customised third-party manufacturing through our manufacturing partners, with dosage forms offered as per plant capability.

Are prescription medicines sold to the public through this website?

No. The product list is meant only for registered healthcare professionals, chemists, distributors and trade partners. This website does not sell medicines to the public.

How do I get terms and territory details?

Send the enquiry form or call us. Territory, terms and prices are confirmed in writing; an enquiry alone does not create a contract.
